Creative, practical, production-minded

I'm Ronald Andrade, a creative designer based in Abu Dhabi with 3+ years of experience across brand identity, web design, social media and motion. I design in Arabic and English, and I take projects from the first concept to the final file, whether that's a website, a content calendar or artwork going to press.

My hands-on print production background means my artwork is always press-ready. Add web, social and motion to that, and I can carry one idea consistently across every channel, from a website to a billboard to a 15-second reel.

Tools Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Figma

  • Brand identity

    Logos, visual systems, typography, colour and branded applications.

  • Web design

    Clean, responsive websites and landing pages, designed for clarity and built around your brand.

  • Social media management

    Content planning, campaign graphics, posts, stories and reels, with a consistent look and a regular posting rhythm.

  • Print and packaging

    Marketing collateral, labels, packaging, signage and production artwork.

  • Motion and video

    Logo animations, promo films and vertical ads, edited and animated in-house.

  • Bilingual design

    Arabic and English layouts that read correctly both ways.

From concept to final print

With hands-on print production experience, I know how a design has to work beyond the screen: layout, resolution, bleed, colour accuracy and artwork the printer can use straight away.

From screen to press: I design for web, social and print, and I know what each one needs.

  • Bleed and safe areas set up for each printer
  • CMYK colour checked before files go to press
  • Arabic and English layouts that read correctly both ways
