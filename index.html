Creative, practical, production-minded

I'm Ronald Andrade, a graphic designer based in Abu Dhabi. I work on brand identity, social media, print and digital design, and I take projects from the first concept through to final production.

  • Brand identity

    Logos, visual systems, typography, colour and branded applications.

  • Social media

    Campaign graphics and platform-ready posts, stories and reels that stay consistent.

  • Print and packaging

    Marketing collateral, labels, packaging, signage and production artwork.

  • Motion and video

    Logo animations, promo films and vertical ads, edited and animated in-house.

Tools Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve

From concept to final print

With hands-on print production experience, I know how a design has to work beyond the screen: layout, resolution, bleed, colour accuracy and artwork the printer can use straight away.

  • Bleed and safe areas set up for each printer
  • CMYK colour checked before files go to press
  • Arabic and English layouts that read correctly both ways
